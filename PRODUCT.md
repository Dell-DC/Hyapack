## Platform

## Users

Primary: B2B brand buyers and procurement/packaging decision-makers at companies (e.g. cosmetics, skincare, lotion, personal-care brands) evaluating HYAPACK as a custom protective-packaging supplier. They arrive needing to be convinced of technical capability (custom moulding to their product dimensions), sustainability credibility, and a clear path to start a custom project.

Secondary (not the primary optimization target for this pass): customers who have already received HYAPACK packaging and want to know how to reuse/repurpose/dispose of it responsibly — served by the separate Multi-Use Guide page.

## Product Purpose

HYAPACK transforms harvested, invasive water hyacinth into custom-moulded, protective packaging that replaces selected single-use plastic packaging (foam, plastic inserts, bubble wrap) for products such as lotion bottles. Success = a prospective brand buyer understands the material story, sees that packaging can be custom-fit to their product via CAD/mould development, trusts the sustainability and sourcing claims (stated conservatively), and takes a next step (explore the Multi-Use Guide or view the packaging / start a custom inquiry).

## Positioning

Custom-moulded protective packaging made from invasive water hyacinth fibre harvested during waterway cleanup — a material story and custom-fit capability a generic "eco packaging" or generic moulded-pulp competitor could not truthfully copy, because it ties directly to a named sourcing community and cleanup activity in Ayutthaya (Bang Sai).

Core line: "Transforming invasive water hyacinth into custom, protective and multi-use packaging."

## Operating Context

- Product lifecycle framing used throughout the site: **Protect → Reuse → Repurpose → Compost**.
- Technical/CAD workflow: customer provides product dimensions/CAD → HYAPACK designs moulded cavity (wall thickness, cushioning zones, locating ribs, neck/base support) → mould & tooling → manufacture.
- Sourcing workflow: **Harvest → Sort → Clean → Dry → Process → Mould → Deliver**, sourced from Ayutthaya (Bang Sai area), partnering with local community enterprise "วิสาหกิจชุมชนบ้านผักตบชวาไม้ตรา", waterway-cleanup groups, municipalities, OTOP enterprises.
- Site is two pages for this pass: a landing/overview page (`index.html`/home route) and a separate, more detailed **Multi-Use Guide** page for reuse/repurpose/dispose instructions. Homepage links out to the guide rather than duplicating its detail.
- English-only content for now; other languages (e.g. Thai) may follow later — do not build a language switcher unless asked.

## Product Principles

1. **Credibility over promotion.** Every environmental or social claim uses the softened, testable phrasing above; nothing is stated as certified, guaranteed, or 100% unless it actually is.
2. **Custom-fit is the technical proof point.** The CAD/mould-development story (customer dimensions → moulded cavity → tooling) is what differentiates HYAPACK from generic eco-packaging and should be given real technical weight, not just a marketing mention.
3. **Lifecycle over single-use.** Protect → Reuse → Repurpose → Compost is the narrative spine connecting the hero, product section, and the separate Multi-Use Guide.
4. **Traceable sourcing, named place.** Ayutthaya/Bang Sai and the community-enterprise partnership are concrete, named details that ground the sustainability story — use them rather than generic "eco-friendly" language.
5. **Real assets only.** Use the six supplied images where they fit; everywhere else, use a clearly marked placeholder rather than inventing photography, names, or data.
